- Advanced Dasha timeline-based gemstone recommendations
- Complete Panchangam & Muhurta calculations
- Transit, Sade Sati & Daily Strength calculations
- Festival & Ekadashi calendar

- Janam Kundli with 9 Vedic planets + outer planets
- 20 Divisional Charts (D1 to D60)
- Special Lagnas (Ghatika, Hora, Bhava, Shree, Indu, Pranapada)
- Chandra & Surya Kundli reference charts
- Jaimini Arudha Padas (A1–A12, AL, UL)
- Vimshottari Dasha Engine (Mahadasha → Antardasha → Pratyantardasha)
- Graha Drishti (Planetary Aspects)
- Ashtakavarga System (BAV & SAV)
- Bhava Chalit Chart (Sripati & Equal House)
- KP Astrology (Krishnamurti Paddhati)
- Kundli Matching (36 Guna Ashtakoota Milan + Mangal Dosha)
- Multi-Ayanamsa support (Lahiri, KP, Raman)
- Hindi & English internationalization (i18n)
